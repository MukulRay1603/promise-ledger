The problem

    report · letter · slides · minutes · agreement

How it works

From a stack of PDFs to one receipt.

  1. UploadDrag in the PDFs

  3. CheckCode matches every quote
  4. ReceiptShare, print or export

The model is one step of eight

  1. CodeRead pages
  2. CodeRedact
  3. AIPropose quotes
  4. CodeVerify on page
  5. CodeSet stage
  6. CodeCompare records
  7. PeopleDecide
  8. OutputReceipt and share
  • No quote on the page, no claim
  • Stages from rules, not the model
  • Neutral labels, no legal findings
  • Only redacted text reaches the model
  • Demo uploads deleted in 24 hours

Stack: Python, FastAPI, pypdf, Gemini with structured output, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run.

Built today, and where it goes next

23 features live today · 326 automated tests · 2 more by the Dec 2 final

  1. Live today

    • Reads every PDF in a deal’s packet
    • Quotes checked word for word on their pages
    • Pages corrected; unverifiable quotes dropped
    • Stages set by rules, from proposed to delivered
    • One-party signing flagged
    • What changed, with word-level edits
    • Missing terms and certificates found
    • Reminders for term ends and certificates
    • Reviewer decisions with name, note and time
    • Printable receipt with a review stamp
    • Read-only share links for residents
    • Excel and CSV export with a preview
    • Guided tour and 8 captioned explainers
    • Redaction, injection guard, sandboxed PDFs
    • Runs without a key; about $0.002 per PDF
    • Keyboard, contrast, larger text, dark mode
    • Names the records to ask for next
    • Ask a deal, for staff and for residents: answers only from checked quotes
    • Ask and listen by voice
    • Reads scanned pages; a person confirms any signature on one
    • Tested on unseen and adversarial records
    • One real public record, checked by hand
    • Changed end dates and amendments caught

  2. Dec 2 final

    • Find more of each record’s commitments: recall was 77% on unseen records and 39% on the real one
    • Feedback and a timed task with housing staff

  3. After

    • Drafts records requests and follow-up letters for staff to approve
    • Alerts before a term ends
    • Spanish and other languages
    • Reads handwriting on scanned pages
    • More cities, and a kit to run it in-house

Every assistant keeps the same rule: it answers only from checked quotes, shows the page, and a person approves anything it sends or saves. Details are in the full guide (opens in a new tab).
