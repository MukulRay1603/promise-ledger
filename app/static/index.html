Data
The sample deals are made up. The County page reads real public records of Prince George's County, Maryland.
Cost
Reading all 811 pages of the county records once cost $0.59 in model use, about $0.73 per 1,000 pages. Each record is read once and cached.

What we keep, and what we don't

  • Not kept: questions asked in Ask a deal and Ask the county are not saved or logged.
  • Kept: deals and records you upload to the public demo (deleted after 24 hours), reviewer decisions with the name and note you enter, and answers to the tester form.
  • Before the model sees text: phone numbers, email addresses and Social Security numbers are removed.
  • No accounts and no ads. Fonts load from Google Fonts; the overview video loads from YouTube only when you press play.

Known limits

  • Council files usually hold the approved agreement, not the signed copy, so most county end dates are counted from the Council's adoption and read "or later".
  • "Not found" means no wording was found in the records read. It is a question to ask, never a finding that a term is missing.
  • Five county agreements state no street address, so they are listed beside the map, not placed on it. Four sites are located to the street only.
  • Scanned pages are transcribed by AI and marked; their quotes should be checked against the PDF.
  • English records only, for now.

The model is one step of eight

  1. CodeRead pages
  2. CodeRedact
  3. AIPropose quotes
  4. CodeVerify on page
  5. CodeSet stage
  6. CodeCompare records
  7. PeopleDecide
  8. OutputReceipt and share
  • No quote on the page, no claim
  • Stages from rules, not the model
  • Neutral labels, no legal findings
  • Only redacted text reaches the model
  • Demo uploads deleted in 24 hours

Stack: Python, FastAPI, pypdf, Gemini with structured output, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Run.
